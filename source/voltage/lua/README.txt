CUTTHROAT CARNIVORE NOTESKIN - VOLTAGE (@VARIANT@)

- Solid, high-visibility arrows: thick dark outline, bright rim and a glow halo,
  so they read clearly on any background.
- Rendered at 4x pixel density, so they stay sharp at 1080p and above.
- BPM indicator: a lightning bolt strikes inside every receptor on each beat and
  decays before the next one, so how fast it flashes tells you the BPM. Beat 1 of
  each measure hits harder. The bolt in every arrow's face strikes on the beat too.
- Hits: star-glint flare, shockwave ring and twinkling sparkles. On the best
  judgments the arrow shatters along glowing cracks and lightning forks out.
- Holds are energy beams with flowing rings; rolls are crackling lightning; holding
  charges a crackling energy orb at the receptor.

- Rhythm is easy to read: every note value has its own colour AND its own face
  symbol, so it still reads if you're colour-blind:
    4th red bolt, 8th blue diamond, 12th purple triangle, 16th yellow circle,
    24th pink hollow triangle, 32nd orange ring, 48th cyan hollow diamond,
    64th green small ring, 192nd grey dot.

Variants: Color (bright full-colour arrows), Pink & Black and Black & Red (black
arrows with a neon frame; the face and inner ring glow in the rhythm colour).
