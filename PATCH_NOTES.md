# Patch notes

## v0.4.0 "Inferno" — Beta Build 1 (11/22/2026)
- New: solid forged-metal arrows in rhythm colours with a heavy black outline.
- New: beat bolt — ON for the first half of every beat, OFF for the second half.
- New: shatter rebuilt — 28 small iron fragments with gravity, plus a bigger debris
  storm (up to 26 slivers), 14 sparks and 12 embers on a Fantastic.
- New: 3 overlapping burst slots per column, so streams stack shatters.
- New: hyper-beam holds (white-hot core, crimson energy, rings flowing on the beat)
  and a charging energy ball with ring pulses at the receptor. Rolls: gold helix beam.
- Changed: smaller flash, heavier smoke.
- Known: the thicker beam (last change) still needs a full in-game check.
