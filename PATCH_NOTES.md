# Patch notes

## v1.0.0 — Official Release (Build 2, 11/22/2026)
- **Voltage is the official default** in the Cutthroat Carnivore theme and in
  NotITGmania Cutthroat Carnivore Edition.
- All 9 variations ship together and are switchable in Player Options → NoteSkin:
  Voltage, Voltage Red, Voltage Pink, Inferno, Inferno Cycle, Forged, Forged Cycle,
  Glossy, Glossy Cycle.
- New: Voltage — fully coloured arrows, a face symbol per rhythm (colour-blind
  friendly), edge colour per direction, receptor beat bolt (beat 1 hits harder),
  shatter + lightning on the best judgments, energy-beam holds, lightning rolls.
- Inferno: thicker hyper-beam core and energy band.
- Source for every variation is in this repo.

## v0.4.0 "Inferno" — Beta Build 1 (11/22/2026)
- New: solid forged-metal arrows in rhythm colours with a heavy black outline.
- New: beat bolt — ON for the first half of every beat, OFF for the second half.
- New: shatter rebuilt — 28 small iron fragments with gravity, plus a bigger debris
  storm (up to 26 slivers), 14 sparks and 12 embers on a Fantastic.
- New: 3 overlapping burst slots per column, so streams stack shatters.
- New: hyper-beam holds (white-hot core, crimson energy, rings flowing on the beat)
  and a charging energy ball with ring pulses at the receptor. Rolls: gold helix beam.
- Changed: smaller flash, heavier smoke.
- Known: the thicker beam (last change) still needs a full in-game check.

## v0.3.0 "Forged" — Beta (11/22/2026)
- Hammered, scratched dark-iron arrows with energy cracks; receptors with a dark
  backing for busy backgrounds; heavier smoke, smaller flash.

## v0.2.0 "Glossy" — Beta (11/22/2026)
- First playable build: glossy armour arrows, lightning-filled faces flashing on the
  beat, 12-shard shatter, lightsaber holds, force-lightning rolls, reactor mines,
  ki-aura receptors.
