# Help make it better

Play a few songs with `cutthroat-carnivore-inferno`, then tell CC in the Discord
(collabs channel) or reply on his posts. Screenshots or a short clip help a ton.

## What to check
1. **Readability at your speed:** can you read stream colours instantly? Jumps?
2. **Beat bolt:** easy to notice? Too much, too little, wrong timing on BPM changes?
3. **Shatter:** epic or distracting? Does it ever hide the next arrows?
4. **Hold / roll beams:** do they look like a hyper beam? Readable in jumps with holds?
5. **Receptors:** easy to see on busy backgrounds and dark ones?
6. **Performance:** any frame drops vs your usual skin? (Say what machine you're on.)
7. **Anything that looks cheap, toy-like or off-brand.**

Format that helps most:
`version · song · speed mod · what you saw · screenshot/clip`
